## Prose rules

- Start every prose sentence with an uppercase letter.
- Use a prose colon only when introducing an explicit definition.
- Preserve required punctuation in code, URLs, mathematical notation, JSON, YAML, and commands.
- Connect adjacent ideas with words that express their actual logical relationship.
- State each sentence’s intended message explicitly and use active verbs.
- Explain unfamiliar ideas before another passage depends on them.
- Keep definitions connected to the example rather than repeating a glossary pattern.
- Remove ceremonial phrasing, decorative metaphors, inflated claims, and commentary about the writing itself.
- Follow the repository’s British English, sentence length, and punctuation rules.
- Before finalising prose, check sentence openings and every prose colon.
