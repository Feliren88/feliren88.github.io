    Check the strength of your answer

    A defensible answer does four things:

    1. Answers the question directly before adding detail.
    2. Explains the mechanism with the module's terms.
    3. States an assumption, trade-off, or failure mode.
    4. Uses a concrete example, equation, or test when the prompt asks for one.

    These are criteria, not a model answer. Compare them with what you wrote, then choose when this question should return.
