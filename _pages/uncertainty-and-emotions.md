<section class="ue-part ue-prose" id="trap">
  <p class="ue-deck">The mind produces doubt. Trouble starts when life must stop until the doubt is resolved.</p>
  <div class="ue-loop" id="ue-loop" role="group" aria-label="The threat-certainty loop">
    <button data-step="0"><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-alarm"/></svg><b>Uncertainty</b><small>something remains unknown</small></button><i>→</i>
    <button data-step="1"><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-wave"/></svg><b>Threat</b><small>the unknown feels dangerous</small></button><i>→</i>
    <button data-step="2"><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-move"/></svg><b>Urgency</b><small>solve it now</small></button><i>→</i>
    <button data-step="3"><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-loop"/></svg><b>Certainty move</b><small>check, review, avoid</small></button><i>→</i>
    <button data-step="4"><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-anchor"/></svg><b>Relief</b><small>brief, but persuasive</small></button>
  </div>
  <p class="ue-loop-read" id="ue-loop-read" role="status">Tap a stage. The loop feels useful because relief is real. Its cost arrives in the next round.</p>
  <div class="ue-shift"><span>Old objective</span><b>uncertainty → certainty</b><strong>becomes</strong><span>Trained objective</span><b>uncertainty → proceed anyway</b></div>
  <p>A threat signal may be a thought, image, memory, sensation, urge, doubt, or a feeling that something is wrong. It is information generated by the mind. It does not automatically deserve the status of fact, prediction, moral verdict, or instruction.</p>
</section>

<section class="ue-part ue-prose" id="moves">
  <h2><span>02</span> Certainty moves</h2>
  <p class="ue-deck">A certainty move is something done mainly to make doubt, fear, guilt, or an intrusive possibility feel resolved.</p>
  <div class="ue-moves">
    <div><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-loop"/></svg><b>Inside the head</b><p>Reviewing memory, replaying a conversation, analysing motives, checking feelings, suppressing a thought, replacing it with a safe one.</p></div>
    <div><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-evidence"/></svg><b>In the world</b><p>Googling, checking again, comparing evidence, confessing for relief, testing a reaction, avoiding a trigger.</p></div>
    <div><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-people"/></svg><b>Borrowed certainty</b><p>Asking someone else to confirm that you are safe, good, correct, forgiven, or unlikely to regret the choice.</p></div>
  </div>
  <p>The behaviour alone does not settle the question. Checking a bill can solve an external problem. Checking it until anxiety gives permission to stop serves a different function. Ask: <b>What am I tempted to do specifically so I can feel sure?</b></p>
  <div class="ue-equation"><span>relief now</span><i>→</i><span>dependence later</span></div>
</section>

<section class="ue-part ue-prose" id="games">
  <h2><span>03</span> The short game and the long game</h2>
  <p class="ue-deck">A certainty move can be locally rational and globally expensive.</p>
  <div class="ue-payoffs">
    <article class="short"><header><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-loop"/></svg><div><small>Strategy C</small><h3>Resolve uncertainty</h3></div></header><div class="now"><span>now</span><b>+5</b><p>Distress falls.</p></div><div class="later"><span>next rounds</span><b>−2</b><p>The mind learns that uncertainty required neutralising.</p></div></article>
    <div class="ue-vs">versus</div>
    <article class="long"><header><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-open"/></svg><div><small>Strategy U</small><h3>Leave it unresolved</h3></div></header><div class="now"><span>now</span><b>−4</b><p>Discomfort remains.</p></div><div class="later"><span>later rounds</span><b>+8</b><p>Uncertainty loses control over behaviour.</p></div></article>
  </div>
  <div class="ue-equation big"><span>best move<sub>short</sub></span><b>≠</b><span>best move<sub>long</sub></span></div>
  <p>Do not repeatedly sacrifice strategic freedom to purchase tactical certainty. The numbers are illustrative payoffs, not clinical measurements.</p>
  <div class="ue-tax"><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-scale"/></svg><div><h3>The certainty tax</h3><p>time resolving + opportunities avoided + attention consumed + dependence created</p></div><b>How much am I willing to pay to know for sure?</b></div>
</section>

<section class="ue-part ue-prose" id="training">
  <h2><span>04</span> Uncertainty training</h2>
  <p class="ue-deck">Approach what the threat system wants you to avoid. Leave the question unresolved. Return to the life in front of you.</p>
  <div class="ue-formula"><div><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-alarm"/></svg><b>Uncertainty</b></div><i>+</i><div><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-open"/></svg><b>Non-resolution</b></div><i>+</i><div><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-move"/></svg><b>Chosen action</b></div><i>=</i><strong>Freedom training</strong></div>
  <ol class="ue-protocol">
    <li><b>Identify the signal.</b><span>“A threat signal has appeared.”</span></li>
    <li><b>Name the uncertainty.</b><span>What if I am wrong? What if I regret this? What if this feeling means something?</span></li>
    <li><b>Find the certainty move.</b><span>What would I do now mainly to feel sure?</span></li>
    <li><b>Leave the question open.</b><span>Maybe. Maybe not. I do not currently know.</span></li>
    <li><b>Allow the response.</b><span>Let doubt, discomfort, fear, or guilt sensations be present.</span></li>
    <li><b>Return to chosen action.</b><span>What would I be doing if I did not need to solve this first?</span></li>
  </ol>
  <div class="ue-success"><span>Success is measured here</span><b>chosen action despite uncertainty</b><small>Anxiety may fall, stay, or rise. Its level does not grade the exercise.</small></div>
</section>

<section class="ue-part ue-prose" id="state">
  <h2><span>05</span> State management</h2>
  <p class="ue-deck">These skills help you stay effective while an intense state is present. They do not promise to remove it.</p>
  <div class="ue-oern" id="ue-oern">
    <button data-copy="Separate the event from the interpretation, threat story, emotion, and action urge."><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-eye"/></svg><b>Observe</b><span>See what is happening.</span></button>
    <button data-copy="Treat discomfort as something you can carry without declaring an emergency."><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-anchor"/></svg><b>Endure</b><span>Remain effective.</span></button>
    <button data-copy="Name the state, check the facts, scale its intensity, then choose the larger game."><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-wave"/></svg><b>Regulate</b><span>Influence the state.</span></button>
    <button data-copy="Balance your objective, the other person's objective, the relationship, and self-respect."><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-people"/></svg><b>Navigate</b><span>Deal with people.</span></button>
  </div>
  <p class="ue-oern-read" id="ue-oern-read" role="status">Choose a state skill to see its operating instruction.</p>
  <div class="ue-three"><p><b>Distress</b> is not proof of danger.</p><p><b>Urgency</b> is not proof of importance.</p><p><b>Emotion</b> is a signal, not a command.</p></div>
  <div class="ue-warning"><b>State management can become another certainty move.</b><p>Use breathing, grounding, or other regulation to remain deliberate, not as a contract that anxiety must disappear before you continue.</p></div>
</section>

<section class="ue-part ue-prose" id="budget">
  <h2><span>06</span> Give uncertainty a budget</h2>
  <p class="ue-deck">Check in proportion to the external problem, not in proportion to anxiety.</p>
  <div class="ue-budget" id="ue-budget">
    <button data-answer="Check the account or receipt once. This is a finite fact in the world."><b>A</b><span>Actionable</span><small>Did I pay the electricity bill?</small></button>
    <button data-answer="Gather decision-relevant evidence, set a deadline, then choose."><b>B</b><span>Researchable</span><small>Which job has better economics?</small></button>
    <button data-answer="No useful amount of analysis can buy this guarantee. Accept residual uncertainty."><b>C</b><span>Unresolvable</span><small>Can I know I will never regret this?</small></button>
  </div>
  <p class="ue-budget-read" id="ue-budget-read" role="status">Select a category.</p>
  <p>A rigid one-check rule can itself become a ritual. Use the amount of evidence the problem requires. Stop when further checking responds to discomfort rather than the world.</p>
</section>

<section class="ue-part ue-prose" id="decisions">
  <h2><span>07</span> Do not confuse new anxiety with new information</h2>
  <p class="ue-deck">Reopen a decision when the world changes, not merely because your internal state changed.</p>
  <div class="ue-decision" id="ue-decision">
    <p>After deciding, what changed?</p>
    <div><button data-decision="evidence"><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-evidence"/></svg>New external evidence</button><button data-decision="anxiety"><svg class="ue-i"><use href="#ue-wave"/></svg>Only anxiety rose</button></div>
    <output id="ue-decision-out">Choose the closest answer.</output>
  </div>
  <div class="ue-voi"><span>keep researching while</span><b>value of more information &gt; cost of delay</b><span>decide when</span><b>value of more information &lt; cost of delay</b></div>
  <p>After deciding, do not reopen the case solely because discomfort remains. Reconsider when genuinely new evidence changes the decision.</p>
</section>

<section class="ue-part ue-prose" id="practice">
  <h2><span>08</span> Build capacity gradually</h2>
  <p class="ue-deck">Start below the top of the ladder. Reckless flooding is not the objective.</p>
  <div class="ue-ladder" aria-label="Example uncertainty ladder">
    <div style="--w:24%"><b>2/10</b><span>Leave a minor message imperfect</span></div>
    <div style="--w:36%"><b>3/10</b><span>Do not reread a routine email</span></div>
    <div style="--w:52%"><b>4–5/10</b><span>Make a small decision without extra research</span></div>
    <div style="--w:70%"><b>6/10</b><span>Approach a meaningful trigger without neutralising</span></div>
    <div style="--w:90%"><b>7–8/10</b><span>Tolerate personally difficult ambiguity</span></div>
  </div>
  <div class="ue-courage"><div><span>Courage</span><b>chosen action despite fear</b></div><div><span>Confidence</span><b>trust in your ability to respond</b></div></div>
  <p>You do not need proof that nothing bad will happen. You need a reasonable reading of the situation, a proportionate response, and enough trust that you can handle what follows.</p>
  <div class="ue-coda" aria-label="The complete uncertainty operating sequence">
    <p>When uncertainty appears</p>
    <div><span><b>Ask the world</b>What does the situation objectively require?</span><i>→</i><span><b>Ask the mind</b>What am I being urged to do only for relief?</span><i>→</i><span><b>Choose the larger game</b>What action serves the life I am building?</span></div>
    <strong>Uncertainty may remain. The day does not have to stop.</strong>
  </div>
