Walk through an embedding space

63,213 ACL paper abstracts, embedded and laid out in 2 dimensions. Zoom in to see finer topics, search for a word to see where it lives, and move the year to watch the field grow.

The map, its density, the topic labels and the paper positions are WizMap's ACL Abstracts dataset, from Wang, Hohman and Chau, WizMap: Scalable Interactive Visualization for Exploring Large Machine Learning Embeddings (ACL 2023 demo; code, MIT licence). Abstracts come from the ACL Anthology. This page shows a seeded sample of 6,000 papers over density computed from all of them. Licences.
