Route, send and address, step by step

Settle a network one router at a time with Dijkstra's algorithm, drop packets and watch TCP's window react, and split an address into network and host bits.

Algorithms as in Kurose and Ross, Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach. Topic outline after Networks Foundation. scripts/verify_explainers.py checks routing against a heap-based Dijkstra, subnets against Python's ipaddress, and the congestion window against a reference trace.
