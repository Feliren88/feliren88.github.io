Train a GAN in your browser

A generative adversarial network (GAN) trains 2 networks against each other. The generator makes points, while the discriminator compares them with the data. Press Train to train the generator here and watch its points approach the data’s shape.

After Kahng, Thorat, Chau, Viégas and Wattenberg, GAN Lab: Understanding Complex Deep Generative Models using Interactive Visual Experimentation (IEEE VIS 2018; code, Apache 2.0). The networks, their backpropagation and Adam are written for this page and checked against finite differences by scripts/verify_explainers.py. Losses follow Goodfellow et al., Generative Adversarial Nets (2014), with the non-saturating generator loss.
