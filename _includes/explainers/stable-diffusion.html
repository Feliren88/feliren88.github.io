Watch Stable Diffusion refine one image

Choose a prompt and a guidance scale, then scrub through the 50 refining steps. The frames are real Stable Diffusion outputs for a fixed seed, so the only things changing are the ones you control.

Frames, latents and noise predictions come from Diffusion Explainer (code, MIT licence) by the Polo Club of Data Science, described in Lee et al., Diffusion Explainer: Visual Explanation for Text-to-image Stable Diffusion (IEEE VIS 2024). This page keeps every second step of seed 1 for 2 of their prompts.
