Watch a trained CNN classify an image

Each square shows a feature map calculated from a small trained network. Follow the guide card. Then hover over a map to trace its inputs, or select it to inspect the calculation.

The network, its trained weights and the sample images are Tiny VGG from CNN Explainer (code, MIT licence) by the Polo Club of Data Science, described in Wang et al., CNN Explainer: Learning Convolutional Neural Networks with Interactive Visualization (IEEE TVCG 2020). The layout follows theirs. Every layer here is checked against NumPy by scripts/verify_explainers.py. Licences.
