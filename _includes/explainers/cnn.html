Watch a trained CNN classify an image

Every square is a real feature map of a small trained network, computed in your browser. Follow the guide card, hover over a map to trace what feeds it, and select a map to see how it is made.

The network, its trained weights and the sample images are Tiny VGG from CNN Explainer (code, MIT licence) by the Polo Club of Data Science, described in Wang et al., CNN Explainer: Learning Convolutional Neural Networks with Interactive Visualization (IEEE TVCG 2020). The layout follows theirs. Every layer here is checked against NumPy by scripts/verify_explainers.py.
