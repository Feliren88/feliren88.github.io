Build it by hand

Diffusion you can compute exactly

Shrink the problem to 2 dimensions and 2 classes of data, and the noise a perfect denoiser would predict has a closed form. Nothing here is trained. Add noise to see the forward process, then remove it to generate, and push the guidance past 1 to see what it costs.

The data is a mixture of 6 Gaussian bumps with standard deviation 0.16, 3 per class. Noise follows the linear schedule of Ho et al. (2020) over 1,000 steps and is removed with 50 deterministic DDIM steps (Song et al., 2021). Guidance follows Ho and Salimans, Classifier-Free Diffusion Guidance (2022). The sampler was checked against an independent NumPy implementation.
