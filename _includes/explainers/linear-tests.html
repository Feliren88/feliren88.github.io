Most named tests are one linear model

A t-test, a correlation and an ANOVA are usually taught as separate tools. Each is a linear model with a particular predictor, and its p-value is the test that one coefficient is 0. Learn the model once and the names become labels. Run a test and its model on the same sample below and compare the outputs.

The same idea, test by test. G is a 0/1 group indicator; G2, G3 are indicators for further groups.
Named testLinear modelWhat the coefficient isRank-based version
One-sample t-testy ~ 1β0 is the mean; test β0 = 0Wilcoxon signed-rank: signed_rank(y) ~ 1
Paired t-testy2 − y1 ~ 1β0 is the mean difference within pairsWilcoxon matched pairs: signed_rank(y2 − y1) ~ 1
Two-sample t-testy ~ 1 + Gβ1 is the difference in group meansMann-Whitney U: rank(y) ~ 1 + G
Welch's t-testy ~ 1 + G, a separate variance per groupβ1 as above, without assuming equal spreadMann-Whitney, as for the two-sample test
Pearson correlationy ~ 1 + xβ1 is the slope; with both variables standardised it equals rSpearman: rank(y) ~ 1 + rank(x)
One-way ANOVAy ~ 1 + G2 + G3 + …Each β is a group's difference from the first; the F-test asks whether all are 0Kruskal-Wallis: rank(y) ~ 1 + G2 + G3 + …
Two-way ANOVAy ~ 1 + A + B + A:BMain effects, plus an interaction term for whether A's effect depends on Bno simple rank version
ANCOVAy ~ 1 + G + xThe group difference after adjusting for a continuous covariateno simple rank version
Chi-square goodness of fitlog(count) ~ 1 + category, PoissonEach β is a category's log ratio to the firstnot applicable
Chi-square independencelog(count) ~ 1 + A + B + A:B, PoissonThe A:B interaction is the dependence being testednot applicable

The rank versions are approximations, close once samples are of moderate size; Lindeløv's simulations give the sizes test by test. Every row inherits the same assumptions: independent observations, and residuals that are roughly normal with equal variance. Checking the residuals of one model checks the test.

After Jonas Kristoffer Lindeløv, Common statistical tests are linear models (or: how to teach stats). The table is condensed in my own words. The panel's statistics are checked against SciPy by scripts/verify_linear_tests.py.
