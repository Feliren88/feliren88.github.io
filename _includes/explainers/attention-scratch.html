Self-attention from scratch, one word at a time

This is the same computation, small enough that you can read every number. One sentence, 16-dimensional embeddings, and the 5 lines of PyTorch that turn a word into a context vector. Choose a query word, then switch the scaling, the mask, extra heads and a second sentence on and off.

The sentence, the sizes (d = 16, dq = dk = 24, dv = 28) and the code follow Sebastian Raschka, Understanding and Coding the Self-Attention Mechanism of Large Language Models From Scratch (2023). The weights come from a seeded generator on this page, so the values differ from the article's PyTorch output while every step and shape matches.
